01 — The squeeze

Allocation windows close faster than LP raises.

Solo GPs win SPV allocations on the cap table, then have weeks to wire. Filling a vehicle from an LP network takes longer, so good allocations get cut down or handed back.

Tight windows

Companies set short closing timelines on oversubscribed rounds and secondaries.

Slow LP commitments

Diligence, docs, and wires take time, even with LPs who want in.

Smaller checks

Without capital on hand, operators commit only what they're sure they can fill.

PECO closes the gap between the wire date and the raise.

Take the full allocation, then fill the SPV on your own timeline.

02 — How it works

Four steps, up to 12 weeks.

Each bridge is secured by the shares it buys and repaid from your SPV's closing. You keep the deal, the LP relationships, and the carry.

  1. 01

    Review

    Share your allocation, closing date, and LP pipeline for underwriting.

  2. 02

    Bridge

    PECO provides bridge financing for the equity purchase so you meet the company's deadline.

  3. 03

    Raise

    You fill the SPV from your LPs. A one-time fee on SPV LPs covers the financing cost.

  4. 04

    Repay

    The SPV closes and repays the bridge. Any unsold shares are retained by PECO.

05 — FAQ

Common questions.

Do I give up economics?

No. The deal and the carry stay with you. Financing is covered by the one-time fee on SPV LPs.

What if the SPV doesn't fully fill?

The SPV repays what it raised, and PECO retains the unsold shares.

How fast can you fund?

We work to the company's closing date. Reach out as soon as the allocation is confirmed.

What do you need to start?

The company, allocation size, closing date, your LP pipeline, and your track record.
