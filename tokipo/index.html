02 — Why tokenize

Tokenization fixes what SPVs break.

Direct payouts

Shares are held in title; smart contracts receive and route distributions at liquidity — no multi-layer SPV payout chains.

Liquidity premium

Liquid wrappers on private names trade rich. Liquidity commands a markup over locked-up SPV interests.

Fractional access

Long-tail investors reach insider allocations at insider prices — small tickets instead of $100K SPV minimums.

Exit demand

Vault demand absorbs the shares PECO retains, turning bridge collateral into liquid inventory.

03 — The engine

PECO: a profitable supply engine.

Primary Equity Credit Opportunities (PECO) provides bridge financing to entrepreneurial SPV operators, secured by the shares they purchase. Every deal ends one of two ways: a high-yield loan repaid, or coveted equity retained at a discount.

  1. 01

    Bridge

    PECO funds the operating partner's equity purchase with a short bridge loan.

  2. 02

    Fee

    Interest is financed by a one-time fee on LPs in the new SPV.

  3. 03

    Buffer

    Operating partners stake 10–20% of funding as first-loss protection.

  4. 04

    Exit

    The SPV closes and repays; unsold equity is retained as vault inventory.
